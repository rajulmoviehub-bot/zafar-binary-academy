Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This notice explains how ZAFAR BINARY ACADEMY collects and uses information when visitors use our website and when students register for online education. We aim to handle personal information carefully and in accordance with applicable data-protection law.

1. Information we collect

  • Registration and contact details: passport name as entered by you, age, email address, WhatsApp number and country.
  • Account and learning records: account identifier, application and enrolment status, attendance, lesson progress, examination submissions and certificate records.
  • Payment administration details: payment status, transaction reference, amount, date and limited payer details needed to verify a payment. Card details should be entered only on the payment provider's secure checkout if one is made available; we do not ask you to send card PINs, passwords or one-time codes to the academy.
  • Technical information: basic device, browser, security and diagnostic information processed by the website and its service providers.

2. Why we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, assess course applications, communicate with students, administer classes and assessments, verify fees, issue and verify academy certificates, respond to support requests, prevent misuse and meet legal obligations.

3. Service providers

Our website and student services may use infrastructure providers, including Vercel for website hosting and Supabase for authentication and database services. If a payment provider is activated, payment information necessary to complete or verify a transaction may also be processed by that provider under its own privacy terms. We do not sell student personal information.

4. International students and transfers

Students may access the academy from different countries. Our service providers may process information in countries outside the student's home country. Where required, we will use appropriate safeguards under applicable data-protection law.

5. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to administer accounts, teaching, payment records, certificates, resolve disputes and meet legal requirements. We use access controls and reasonable technical and organisational safeguards. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and requests

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or raise an objection or complaint. Some records may need to be retained where required for legal, accounting, security or dispute purposes. Contact us using the email below and describe your request.

7. Children

Paid course registration is intended for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly accept student registrations from people under 18.

8. Cookies and session storage

Authentication and website features may use browser storage or similar technologies to keep a student signed in, protect sessions and support essential website functions. Blocking these technologies may prevent login or student features from working correctly.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact rajulmoviehub@gmail.com.

Do not email your password, one-time password, payment PIN, full card number or card security code.
