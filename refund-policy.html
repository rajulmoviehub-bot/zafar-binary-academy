Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026 · Applies to course and membership payments made to ZAFAR BINARY ACADEMY.

ZAFAR BINARY ACADEMY aims to describe its course services and fees clearly. This policy explains how to request a refund or report a payment problem. It does not limit any consumer rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under the laws applicable to a student.

1. When you may request a refund

  • Duplicate payment: If you were charged more than once for the same course enrolment, contact us with the payment references. A verified duplicate payment will be refunded.
  • Payment received but enrolment not provided: If we verify that your payment was received but we cannot provide the course access you purchased, we will investigate and, where the service cannot be supplied, arrange an appropriate refund.
  • Academy cancellation: If the academy cancels a course and cannot offer a reasonable replacement date or equivalent service, you may request a refund for the undelivered course service.
  • Change of mind before course access: You may request a refund within 7 calendar days of payment if you have not accessed paid course materials or attended a live teaching session. Requests are reviewed against the enrolment and access records.

2. When a refund may not be available

Once paid digital course materials have been accessed or live teaching has been provided, a change-of-mind refund may not be available. Missing a class, not completing lessons, dissatisfaction with simulated practice results, or not making profits from trading are not by themselves grounds for a refund. This does not affect refunds required by applicable law or the remedies described above.

3. How to request a refund

  1. Email rajulmoviehub@gmail.com using the email address on your student account.
  2. Use the subject line Refund Request – ZAFAR BINARY ACADEMY.
  3. Include your full name, registered email, course, payment date, amount and payment transaction/reference number, plus a short explanation.
  4. For your safety, never email your password, PIN, one-time password, full card number or card security code.

4. Review and processing

We aim to acknowledge a complete request within 3 business days and provide an outcome within 10 business days. If approved, the refund will normally be sent to the original payment method where the payment provider supports it. The payment provider or bank may take additional time to show the funds. We will contact you if more information is needed.

5. Payment verification

A payment screenshot or message alone does not confirm settlement. We verify transactions against the payment provider's records before approving enrolment or a refund. Do not make payments through unofficial links or personal accounts.

6. Contact

Refund and payment support: rajulmoviehub@gmail.com.

ZAFAR BINARY ACADEMY provides education and a virtual-funds practice simulator. The simulator is not a real-money trading account, and course fees do not purchase trading capital or guarantee trading results.
