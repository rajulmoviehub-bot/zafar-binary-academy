Student Rules & Regulations

These rules are always visible to students and visitors. Every student is expected to follow them.

1. Respect everyone

Treat instructors, staff and fellow students respectfully. Harassment, threats, bullying and abusive language are not allowed.

2. Attend classes

Join scheduled Zoom lessons on time, participate appropriately and inform the academy when you cannot attend.

3. Protect your account

Use your own account, keep your password private and never share login access.

4. Protect learning materials

Do not copy, screenshot, photograph, screen-record, resell, publish or distribute paid lessons, recordings, notes, exam papers or student-only resources without written permission.

5. Be honest in exams

Cheating, impersonation, plagiarism, sharing exam answers or interfering with another student's submission are prohibited.

6. Meet exam deadlines

Follow exam instructions and submit your own work before the deadline. Contact the academy promptly if a genuine problem prevents submission.

7. Keep groups professional

Use academy WhatsApp groups for learning and official communication. No spam, scams, unsolicited advertising or sharing private information.

8. Use practice tools responsibly

The simulator uses virtual funds for education only. Never present simulated results as real profits or guaranteed returns.

9. Give accurate information

Keep registration details truthful and up to date. Do not impersonate anyone or submit forged documents or payment evidence.

10. Respect certificate integrity

Certificates are issued only after course completion and assessment requirements are met. Do not alter or falsely present a certificate.

Consequences of Breaking the Rules

Consequences depend on the seriousness of the conduct, available evidence, whether it is repeated, and the student's explanation. They may include:

  1. Reminder or warning for a minor or first-time breach.
  2. Temporary restriction from a class, group or student resources while a concern is reviewed.
  3. Academic action such as an invalid exam submission or supervised re-assessment when cheating or plagiarism is established.
  4. Suspension or dismissal for serious, repeated or deliberate misconduct.
  5. Certificate revocation if a certificate was obtained through fraud or misrepresentation; its online status may be changed to REVOKED.

For significant decisions, the student will be informed and given a reasonable opportunity to explain or appeal to the academy administration. Any action will be proportionate and consistent with applicable law. This section does not create automatic refunds or extra fees; fee matters are handled under the published enrolment terms and applicable law.

These rules help maintain a safe, respectful and honest learning environment. They do not replace the full Terms & Conditions or applicable law.
